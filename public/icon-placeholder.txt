App icons are generated in CI, not edited by hand.

Source images live in resources/:
  resources/icon.png          1024x1024, no transparency, no rounded corners
  resources/splash.png        2732x2732
  resources/splash-dark.png   2732x2732

The build-ipa workflow runs `npx capacitor-assets generate --ios` after
`npx cap add ios`, which writes ios/App/App/Assets.xcassets/. Editing that
folder directly is pointless -- the ios/ project is regenerated every build.

To change the icon, replace resources/icon.png and push.
